ENG# CrowdLuma 
CrowdLuma turns stadium stands into a giant screen: each spectator scans a QR code on their seat, their smartphone displays the right color at the right moment, creating a collective light choreography. No app to install. Open source, it also includes in-seat drink ordering and live audience voting.
